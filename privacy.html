Privacy Policy

JLPT Map · Last updated 17 November 2026

JLPT Map is a Japanese grammar app: a board of cards that explain Japanese grammar from N5 to N1, with quizzes to check yourself. This page explains what the app keeps and where. JLPT Map is operated by Cheung Pak Wai, and “we” and “us” mean us.

JLPT Map collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, it does not connect to our servers or anyone else’s, and nothing you do in it leaves your device.

What stays on your device

To pick up where you left off, the app keeps a few things in its own storage on your phone or tablet:

  • the JLPT level you chose, and your display settings;
  • the board you were looking at and how far you were zoomed in;
  • the cards you added to a quiz, and the questions you answered wrong, so they can be shown again.

This is never sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app, and it is included in your device’s own backups in the same way as any other app’s data.

What we don’t do

JLPT Map has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers. All of the cards, examples and translations are built into the app, so reading them, searching them and taking quizzes works without an internet connection and without contacting any server. The app asks for no permissions: not your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

We do not sell data, because we do not have any.

The App Store and Google Play

You download JLPT Map from Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Those stores handle the download and may collect information under their own privacy policies; we do not receive personal information from them about you.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app handles information differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released.
