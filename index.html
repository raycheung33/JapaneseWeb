JLPT Map

JLPT Map lays out the whole of Japanese grammar on one board, from N5 to N1, so you can see where every point fits.

The board starts with sentences, words, writing, politeness and patterns. Tap a card to zoom into it: verbs open into their groups and forms, particles into their roles, compound sentences into the ways clauses join. Every card is marked with its JLPT level, so you can show only what your exam needs.

Each card explains one point in a few plain sentences, with a table of forms where the grammar changes, and example sentences with furigana, romaji and a translation. Each piece of an example is coloured by the card that explains it.

Add cards to a quiz and test yourself on what they mean, how they are written and which piece of a sentence is missing. The questions you get wrong wait for you at the top of the board until you have them right.

The cards are explained in English, Korean, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Vietnamese, Thai and Hindi.
